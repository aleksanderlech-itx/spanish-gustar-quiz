**Title:** New activity: Reflexive verbs

**Content:**

```
New on Spanish Quizzes: reflexive verbs.

Me levanto, te duchas, se acuerda. The pronoun always matches the subject. It goes before a conjugated verb, or attaches to an infinitive or a gerund: voy a ducharme, estoy duchándome.

Some verbs change meaning with se:
llamar (to call) → llamarse (to be called)
ir (to go) → irse (to leave)
dormir (to sleep) → dormirse (to fall asleep)
```

## Cycle 3 · Present perfect · Mon 19 Oct 2026

### Post (feed) — `present-perfect-feed.mp4`

**Title:** New activity: Present perfect

**Content:**

```
New on Spanish Quizzes: the present perfect.

He hablado, has comido, ha vivido. Use haber + the past participle for what you have done.

-ar verbs → -ado (hablado)
-er and -ir verbs → -ido (comido, vivido)

The irregular ones to learn: hecho, dicho, visto, escrito, vuelto, abierto.

Two traps: the helper verb is always haber, never tener, and the participle never changes (ella ha comido).
```

## Cycle 4 · Present continuous · Mon 2 Nov 2026

### Post (feed) — `present-continuous-feed.mp4`

**Title:** New activity: Present continuous

**Content:**

```
New on Spanish Quizzes: the present continuous.

Estoy hablando, estás comiendo, está viviendo. Use estar + the gerund for what is happening right now.

-ar verbs → -ando (hablando)
-er and -ir verbs → -iendo (comiendo, viviendo)

The irregular ones to learn: leyendo, durmiendo, pidiendo, diciendo, yendo, oyendo.

Pronouns go before estar or attach to the gerund, with an accent: lo estoy leyendo = estoy leyéndolo.
```
