# Activity launch posts

One grammar mini-lesson per new activity from the roadmap (issue #65), one activity
every 2 weeks. Each lesson has two clips, rendered by `npm run render:lessons` from
`src/lessons/`:

| Clip | Size | Where |
|---|---|---|
| `<id>-reel.mp4` | 1080×1920 (9:16) | Instagram and Facebook Reel |
| `<id>-feed.mp4` | 1080×1350 (4:5) | Instagram and Facebook feed post |

## Publishing plan

The Reel goes out on release day (Monday) for reach. The feed post follows on the
Thursday as a reminder that carries the quiz question. Both are published at
**12:00 Europe/Madrid** on Instagram and Facebook.

| Date | Time (Madrid) | Cycle | Activity | Issue | Format | Clip |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mon 19 Oct 2026 | 12:00 CEST | 2 | Reflexive verbs | #68 | Reel | `reflexive-verbs-reel.mp4` |
| Thu 22 Oct 2026 | 12:00 CEST | 2 | Reflexive verbs | #68 | Feed post | `reflexive-verbs-feed.mp4` |
| Mon 2 Nov 2026 | 12:00 CET | 3 | Present perfect | #69 | Reel | `present-perfect-reel.mp4` |
| Thu 5 Nov 2026 | 12:00 CET | 3 | Present perfect | #69 | Feed post | `present-perfect-feed.mp4` |
| Mon 16 Nov 2026 | 12:00 CET | 4 | Present continuous | #70 | Reel | `present-continuous-reel.mp4` |
| Thu 19 Nov 2026 | 12:00 CET | 4 | Present continuous | #70 | Feed post | `present-continuous-feed.mp4` |

Madrid moves from CEST to CET on Sun 25 Oct, so 12:00 Madrid is 10:00 UTC in October
and 11:00 UTC in November.

Rules:

- Publish only once the activity is live on spanish-quizz.es, meaning its issue is
  closed and the production deploy has run. If a release slips, its Reel and post slip
  by the same number of days, and the later cycles keep their own dates unless they slip
  too.
- Pin the comment answering the quiz question on the feed post a day after posting.
- The clips are in `exports/activity-launch-videos.zip`.

**Title:** New activity: Reflexive verbs

**Content:**

```
New on Spanish Quizzes: reflexive verbs.

Me levanto, te duchas, se acuerda. The pronoun always matches the subject. It goes before a conjugated verb, or attaches to an infinitive or a gerund: voy a ducharme, estoy duchándome.

Some verbs change meaning with se:
llamar (to call) → llamarse (to be called)
ir (to go) → irse (to leave)
dormir (to sleep) → dormirse (to fall asleep)

**Title:** New activity: Present perfect

**Content:**

```
New on Spanish Quizzes: the present perfect.

He hablado, has comido, ha vivido. Use haber + the past participle for what you have done.

-ar verbs → -ado (hablado)
-er and -ir verbs → -ido (comido, vivido)

The irregular ones to learn: hecho, dicho, visto, escrito, vuelto, abierto.

Two traps: the helper verb is always haber, never tener, and the participle never changes (ella ha comido).
