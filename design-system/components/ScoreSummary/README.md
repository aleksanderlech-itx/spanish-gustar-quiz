The results score card and mistake notebook shown at the end of a round. Shows real round totals and what to review.

- Score card: an editorial surface with the number in `score-term`, "of N correct" in 17px `muted`, and a 6px `sage` track.
- Mistake notebook: a `panel` surface with editorial corners and `clay-soft` chips (`border-ink` edge, `ink` text).
- Keep "Practise mistakes" whenever saved misses remain, even after a perfect round. Never divide by zero; show an explicit empty result.
- **Consumer provides:** the score, total, review areas and the actions.
